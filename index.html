First-timer playbook

Buying your first car, step by step

The short version of what experienced buyers wish they knew the first time.

1

Set an out-the-door budget

MSRP is just the start. Add freight, PDI, dealer fees, and 13% HST in Ontario — then get insurance quotes before you fall in love with a car.

2

New vs. used is a math question

A 3-year-old Civic or Corolla can save you thousands and still be reliable. Compare the used listing price against a new MSRP here first.

3

Decode the VIN before you visit

Make sure the trim, engine, and model year in the ad match the factory record. If they don't, walk away.

4

Test-drive twice

Once for the thrill, once for the boring stuff: parking, blind spots, rear seats, road noise at highway speed.

5

Negotiate the out-the-door price

Only one number matters: the total you pay to drive away. Get it in writing before you sign anything.

6

Sleep on it

A good deal today is still a good deal tomorrow. Pressure to "sign now" is a red flag, not a discount.
